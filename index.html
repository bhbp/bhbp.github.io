Agile / Scrum delivery

Agile / Scrum Delivery

Agile is more than a buzzword, it's a way of thinking. It's removing blockers, affording time for bug bashing and handling tech debt. It's daily scrums that are beneficial, not just a 15 minute time suck.

01
Lean Ceremonies
Only have meetings that move work forward. Start on time, end on time. Be present.
02
Refined Backlogs
User Stories are refined, estimated, bundled into sprints BEFORE the sprint starts. Let's slice that triangle! 
03
Iterative Delivery
Ship early, ship often. The benefit of live market feedback to plan your next move is unprecedented. 
Collaborative execution

Collaborative Execution

There are many different team structures. Some are basic – PM, Designer, Engineer. Others make decisions by a committee of 20 people. Collaboration requires prioritization, in all scenarios.

01
One Shared Vision
We rally around a shared vision of the Product's purpose, the impact, and our place in the market. The North Star. 
02
Clear Working Agreements
Meeting cadences, peer review process, milestones & checkpoints – PTO coverage. Never assume...discuss. 
03
Consistent Alignment
Project Kick-Offs are great to rally a team. But revisiting our agreements & patterns often helps us stay aligned.
