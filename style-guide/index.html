Voice

Proof Over Adjectives

First person, confident and specific — a senior PM talking to a peer, not a résumé.

Do
  • Lead with the number, then the impact.
  • Short declaratives, one idea each.
  • Verb-first CTAs: "See the Work".
Don't
  • "Passionate", "rockstar", "synergy".
  • Emoji, anywhere.
  • More than one gradient word per headline.
